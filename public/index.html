About

01 — Biography

Aira Edward is a Dubai-based open-format DJ with eleven years behind the decks.

Her sets move between House, Nu Disco, Hip-Hop & R&B Edits, Dancehall, Baile Funk, Jersey Club, UK Garage and DNB. She reads the room and builds from there — which is why she works equally well in a premium venue and in a room full of ravers at 2am.

She holds residencies across Dubai’s premium hospitality circuit — Conrad, Park Hyatt, Jumeirah, Ritz-Carlton, Mövenpick, Hilton — and has performed for Nissan, Erborian, Wingstop, Jetour and Knauf.

fr. the bloc

02 — The Bloc

In 2026, she founded fr. the bloc, a creative community and music collective based in Dubai.

Its debut event, Bloc Zero, packed The Hungry Monkey to full capacity. As the founder, she curates the artist lineups, holds the main residency, and directs the creative vision.

Booking her means booking someone who doesn’t just play rooms — she builds them.
